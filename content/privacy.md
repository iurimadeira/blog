This site does not use analytics, advertising cookies, or user accounts.

The contact form collects your name, email address, and message only so I can read and respond to your enquiry. Web3Forms processes submissions and hCaptcha helps prevent spam. Those providers may process technical data such as your IP address and may retain submission data for up to three years under their current terms.

Do not submit passwords, financial information, health information, or other sensitive data. You can avoid the form and contact me directly at [iurimadeira@gmail.com](mailto:iurimadeira@gmail.com).

To request access to or deletion of a message you submitted, email [iurimadeira@gmail.com](mailto:iurimadeira@gmail.com).

Provider policies: [Web3Forms Privacy Policy](https://web3forms.com/privacy), [Web3Forms Data Processing Agreement](https://web3forms.com/dpa), and [hCaptcha Privacy Policy](https://www.hcaptcha.com/privacy).
